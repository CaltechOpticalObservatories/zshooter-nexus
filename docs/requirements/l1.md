# L1 — Science and facility requirements

## Spectroscopy

- **Wavelength coverage.** ZShooter shall provide simultaneous spectroscopic wavelength coverage
  spanning 0.31–2.45 µm. Continuous coverage across atmospheric gaps is not required.

- **Spatial resolution.** ZShooter shall support natural seeing and delivered seeing-enhanced
  point-spread functions down to 0.3 arcsecond FWHM.

- **Slit length.** ZShooter shall support 10 arcsecond long-slit spectroscopy.

- **Spectral resolution.** ZShooter shall deliver a mean resolving power of at least 18,000 under
  median natural-seeing conditions and 40,000 under supported seeing-enhanced conditions, using a
  slit matched to the delivered image quality.

- **Sensitivity.** At blaze peak and R = 1,000, ZShooter shall achieve a signal-to-noise ratio of at
  least 5 per resolution element in one hour of on-target integration for an AB-magnitude 24.5 point
  source under supported seeing enhancement and an AB-magnitude 23.5 point source under natural
  seeing. Verification in OH-affected regions and K band shall use the adopted sky-line and
  emissivity model.

- **Stability.** ZShooter shall limit instrument-contributed radial-velocity error, for spectra with
  sufficient photon-limited radial-velocity information, to the greater of 200 m/s or 1/60 of a
  native resolution element when reduced using calibration data acquired within four hours of the
  science exposure. For spectra reduced to R = 1,000 using calibration data acquired within 48
  hours, the absolute radial-velocity error shall not exceed 7.5 km/s.

## Operations

- **Operational efficiency.** ZShooter shall be available for timely and remote follow-up
  observation of transient sources, enabling science exposure initiation of a prepared ToO
  observation within five minutes after delivery of the target beam. A single operator shall be able
  to configure the instrument, acquire and guide on the target, control exposures and sequences,
  obtain calibrations, and assess data quality. Supported operations include user-selectable nod
  sequences and independent exposure and readout cadences among spectrograph channels.

- **Lifetime.** ZShooter shall have an operational lifetime of seven years at WMKO before requiring
  major refurbishment.

## Imaging

- **Imaging capability.** ZShooter shall offer at least three simultaneous imaging bands with
  sufficient field for 1% relative photometry across 80% of the observable sky in median-seeing
  conditions. The required bands are u, i, and one selectable intermediate band.

- **High-speed imaging.** ZImager shall support windowed fast-mode imaging at ≥100 Hz, with a goal
  of ≥1 kHz, in a subfield containing the science target and at least one comparison star, in at
  least two bands simultaneously.

- **Absolute timing.** ZImager shall provide GPS-synchronized per-frame absolute timing with
  accuracy ≤1 ms, with a goal of ≤50 µs.

- **Differential photometry.** ZImager shall deliver ≤1% differential photometric precision in
  high-speed mode for sources with per-frame S/N > 50, using an in-field comparison star.

- **Limiting magnitude.** Under median natural-seeing conditions, ZImager shall achieve a 5σ
  point-source detection in a 15-minute exposure to 26 AB mag in each supported imaging band.

## Calibration and data

- **Spectrophotometric fidelity.** Reduced data shall preserve relative flux calibration among
  spectrograph channels and between the spectrograph and each imaging band, with systematic error no
  greater than 10%, and shall support absolute normalization using imaging or spectrophotometric
  standards.

- **Data products.** ZShooter shall automatically produce baseline reduced products for every
  supported observing mode.

- **OH suppression.** ZShooter shall facilitate observations between bright night-sky lines and
  suppress stray light from those lines.

- **Low-resolution capability.** ZShooter shall provide spectra binned to a resolving power of 1,000
  from every spectroscopic configuration.
