# Requirements

ZShooter combines simultaneous 0.31–2.45 µm spectroscopy, rapid transient response, and high-speed
three-band imaging. Its requirements set the sensitivity, resolution, timing, calibration, and
operational capabilities the design must deliver.

- **[L1 — Science and facility requirements](requirements/l1.md):** the required observing
  capabilities and performance.
- **[L2 — Instrument requirements](requirements/l2.md):** their allocation across the instrument and
  observatory interfaces.
